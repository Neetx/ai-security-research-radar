## The 008 reversal (and why it matters beyond 008)
Yesterday's report pre-announced that 008 would move to `dormant` today absent a
slopsquatting item. The check run to confirm that found **two uncaptured
primaries** instead, so the condition was never met:
- [Churilov, 2605.17062](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17062) — 5 frontier models,
  199,845 prompts; **127 package names all five invent identically**, and after
  coordinated disclosure with PyPI Security and Socket.dev **53 remain
  registrable**. One malicious registration reaches every provider's users.
- [Okwor / Orygn, Zenodo DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21199427) (07-05) —
  the first **counter-result**: all 1,641 invented names are still unregistered;
  of 149 publicly-circulated hallucinated names, 22 are registered and none are
  attacks. Hallucination is exposure, not realized harm.

Both **predate 07-14**, so `last_evidence` mechanically stays 07-14 and the
21-day counter still reads "quiet" — while the axis plainly was not. **`last_evidence`
measures the radar's capture recency, not the field's activity.** Neither item was
reachable by a standing lane: 2605.17062's v1 predates every tracked arXiv window
and its v2 was a revision, not an announce; the Orygn paper lives on Zenodo, a
venue no lane touches. Held at seed despite five independent groups, because the
two findings pull in opposite directions and that tension is the honest state.
This is the **second time in six days** (007 on 07-31) that a pending-dormancy
check produced fresh evidence — a pattern, not a coincidence.
